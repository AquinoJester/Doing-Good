Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about orders, payment, shipping, returns, and fit.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, choose your size and product, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout. After you complete payment, you will receive an order confirmation email with the details for your purchase.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Order changes or cancellations are only possible before the item is packed or dispatched. If you need help, contact our support team as soon as possible with your order number and the item you want to change.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept cashless payment methods available through checkout, including GCash and secure card payment options supported by the store. Please check the checkout page for the latest available methods.

How can I check my order status?

You will receive email updates as your order moves from confirmation to dispatch and delivery. If you need an update, contact us with your order number and we will help review the current status.

Where do you ship, and how long does delivery take?

We currently ship across the Philippines. Standard delivery times vary by area, and full shipping details are available in Shipping & Returns.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping fees depend on delivery area and order value. Orders above a qualifying minimum may qualify for free shipping, while smaller purchases are charged a flat rate based on location. Full details are listed in Shipping & Returns.

How do I return or exchange an item?

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for eligible items in original condition with tags and packaging. Please see Shipping & Returns for the full return process and requirements.

How do I choose the right size?

Measure your body according to the garment guide and compare your results to the size chart. Our Size Guide provides measurement references for tops and bottoms.

What should I do if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact our support team within 7 days of delivery and include your order number, a few photos of the item, and a brief description of the issue. We will review the case and help with the next step.
