ABOUT

Impact Report

A straightforward snapshot of how we are improving our materials, processes, and customer experience.

Materials

We continue to review our fabric choices with a focus on comfort, longevity, and responsible sourcing for everyday essentials.

Production

We work with partners who prioritize consistent quality, cleaner handling, and better production standards over excess waste.

People

Our approach values transparent communication, respectful partnerships, and product decisions that support real everyday wear.

Next steps

We are continuing to improve our sourcing process, packaging decisions, and product education to make our values easier to understand and act on.

Summary

Our current focus is on making better decisions in the materials we select, the production standards we expect, and the experience our customers have from browsing to delivery. We are working steadily to improve what we can verify and to communicate clearly where we are making progress and where more work is still needed.
