ABOUT

Sustainability

We design with care, choosing materials and practices that help our products feel better in use and lighter on the planet.

Materials

  • We focus on natural, durable fibers and comfortable everyday fabrics that are easy to wear and easy to care for.
  • Our product categories prioritize materials chosen for softness, longevity, and a lower impact without sacrificing the feel customers expect from premium essentials.

Making and packaging

  • We work with production partners who follow basic quality standards and care about consistent finishing, responsible handling, and practical craftsmanship.
  • Packaging is kept simple and functional, with a focus on reducing excess materials wherever possible and choosing reusable or recyclable options when available.

People and progress

  • We believe good production starts with fair treatment, clear communication, and quality working conditions across the supply chain.
  • Our sustainability approach is reviewed regularly so we can improve product design, sourcing decisions, and operational practices as the business grows.
