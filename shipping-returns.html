Shipping & Returns

Please review the current delivery and return terms before placing an order.

Delivery areas and times

We currently ship across the Philippines. Standard delivery typically takes 3 to 7 business days for Metro Manila orders and 5 to 10 business days for provincial areas, depending on courier routes and local conditions.

Shipping fees

Shipping fees are calculated based on your delivery area and order size. Orders above ₱2,500 may qualify for free shipping within selected locations. Customers can confirm the exact amount at checkout before completing payment.

Return window

Customers may return items within 30 days after delivery. The item must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition, with all tags and original packaging included. A receipt or order confirmation is required for all returns. Personalized, final-sale, or heavily used items may not be eligible for return. Refunds will be processed after the returned item has been received and inspected.

How to return an item

  1. Contact customer service with your order number and the item you want to return or exchange.
  2. Package the item carefully in its original condition with tags and proof of purchase included.
  3. Send the package using the approved return method, and refunds or exchanges will be processed after inspection.
