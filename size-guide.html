How to measure

  • Chest: Measure across the fullest part of the chest while keeping the tape level and comfortably snug.
  • Length: For tops, measure from the highest shoulder point to the desired hemline.
  • Waist and hip: For bottoms, measure across the natural waist and then across the fullest hip area.
  • These are garment measurements, so if you prefer a looser fit, choose the next size up. If you want a closer fit, match your body measurement to the listed garment size.
